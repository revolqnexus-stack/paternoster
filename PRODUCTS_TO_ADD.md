## After Adding Products

1. Create collections: https://admin.shopify.com/store/pater-noster-jwjbtasv/collections
   - Fiction
   - Non-Fiction
   - Poetry
   - LRB Merchandise
   - Staff Picks
   - Audiobooks

2. Assign products to collections (use Tags as criteria for automatic assignment)

3. Configure theme: https://pater-noster-jwjbtasv.myshopify.com/admin/themes/208205676885/editor
   - Set featured product in Hero section
   - Link collections in "Shop By Type" section
   - Customize promotional banners
